Know your cooking oil

What is Sunflower Oil?

Sunflower oil is an edible vegetable oil extracted from the seeds of the sunflower plant (Helianthus annuus). It has a generally mild taste and is commonly used in home cooking, dressings and food preparation.

🌻

From sunflower seeds

Made from sunflower seeds, not from the flower petals. The oil is obtained from the seed kernels.

🪵

Wood pressed process

Your product label identifies the oil as wood pressed and made using the traditional Ghani process.

🍽️

Mild everyday flavour

Its relatively neutral taste can complement ingredients without overpowering the natural flavour of food.

In your kitchen

How can you use Sunflower Oil?

A versatile edible oil for a range of everyday recipes. Choose cooking temperature and duration sensibly, and avoid overheating or repeatedly reusing oil.

🥗

Salads & dressings

Use in homemade dressings, marinades and dips when you want a mild-tasting oil.

🥘

Daily cooking

Suitable for sautéing vegetables, preparing dals, curries, gravies and other everyday dishes.

🍳

Shallow cooking

Can be used for pan cooking and shallow frying. Keep the oil below its smoking point.

🫓

Home-style recipes

Use in suitable savoury recipes such as poha, upma, parathas and snacks according to your cooking method.

🍰

Baking

Its mild flavour can work in selected cakes, muffins and other recipes that call for liquid oil.

🍲

Family meals

Use as one cooking-oil option in a varied, balanced diet. Use moderate portions as all edible oils are energy-dense.

Who can use it?

A practical choice for everyday kitchens

Sunflower oil is an edible cooking ingredient for people who enjoy its taste and cooking characteristics.

May suit

  • Households looking for a mild-flavoured cooking oil.
  • People preparing everyday vegetables, dals, curries and dressings.
  • Home cooks who prefer a wood-pressed oil and understand its flavour and handling characteristics.

Use with care

  • Anyone with a known sunflower-seed or sunflower-oil allergy should avoid it unless advised otherwise by their clinician.
  • People following a medically prescribed diet should follow their healthcare professional's advice.
  • Use reasonable portions; oil is calorie-dense even when it contains mostly unsaturated fats.

Which nutrients matter?

  • Unsaturated fats: include monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fatty acids.
  • Linoleic acid (Omega-6): a common essential fatty acid in conventional sunflower oil. Your label does not give a separate Omega-6 quantity.
  • Oleic acid (Omega-9): a monounsaturated fatty acid naturally present in sunflower oil.
  • Vitamin E: sunflower oil naturally contains tocopherols, including vitamin E; the exact amount in this product is not stated on the supplied label.
Sunflower oil comes in different fatty-acid types, including high-linoleic and high-oleic. Do not describe this product as high-oleic or claim a precise Omega-6 percentage unless supported by a product-specific lab test.

Keep it fresh

Storage & Smart Usage

🌤️

Away from sunlight

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, as directed on your bottle label.

🫙

Close after use

Keep the cap tightly closed and avoid introducing water, food particles or other contaminants into the bottle.

♨️

Do not overheat

Do not allow oil to smoke. Avoid repeated reheating and discard oil that has darkened, foamed or developed an off smell.

Customer questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Aroga Sunflower Oil suitable for daily cooking?

It can be used for everyday cooking such as sautéing, dals, curries and dressings. Use moderate heat, avoid smoking the oil and follow your dietary needs.

Does sunflower oil contain Omega-6?

Conventional sunflower oil commonly contains linoleic acid, an Omega-6 fatty acid. The exact Omega-6 content of this Aroga product is not stated on the supplied label and should not be guessed.

Is this a high-oleic sunflower oil?

The supplied label does not identify it as high-oleic. A product-specific fatty-acid laboratory report is needed before making that claim.

Can I use it for deep frying?

Deep-frying suitability depends on the oil's fatty-acid profile, processing and temperature control. Since the label does not identify the sunflower-oil type, avoid making a blanket high-temperature or repeated-frying claim. Do not overheat or repeatedly reuse oil.

Can sunflower oil be used for hair or massage?

This page describes Aroga Foods Sunflower Oil as an edible cooking oil. Follow the product's intended food use and do not market it as a hair or massage oil.

How should I store the bottle?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, close the cap after use and follow the consume-within period on the label.
